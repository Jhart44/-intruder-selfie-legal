# Privacy Policy – Intruder Selfie

_Last updated: 2026-11-15_

**Summary:** Intruder Selfie keeps everything on your phone. We don't collect, upload, sell or share any data.

## What the app does
When someone enters an incorrect PIN, password or pattern on your phone, the app takes one photo with the front camera and stores it privately inside the app. With the optional Pro upgrade, the app takes a short burst of front-camera photos and one back-camera photo.

## Data and permissions
- **Camera:** used only to take the photos described above.
- **Device Administrator (watch login attempts):** used only so Android can notify the app of a failed unlock attempt. The app cannot lock, wipe or change your device.
- **Notifications:** used to tell you that a photo was captured. Notification contents are hidden on the lockscreen.
- **Photos:** stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not saved to your gallery, not uploaded, and not accessible to other apps. Viewing them requires your fingerprint, face or device PIN. You can delete them at any time in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes them.
- **Sharing (Pro):** with Pro you can share a photo using your phone's share menu. This happens only when you choose to, and you pick where it goes; the app itself never sends photos anywhere.
- **Purchases (Pro):** the optional one-time Pro upgrade is sold and processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details or personal information; Google handles the transaction under its own terms and privacy policy. The app itself has no internet permission; the purchase is carried out by the Google Play Store app on your phone.

## Data we collect
None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or network access, and nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share a photo yourself (Pro).

## Your responsibility
Use the app only on a device you own. Laws about photographing people vary by country; you are responsible for using the app lawfully.

## Children
The app is intended for people aged 18 and over. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18 (the app collects no data at all).
