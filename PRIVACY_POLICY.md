# Privacy Policy – Intruder Selfie

_Last updated: 2026-10-07_

**Summary:** Intruder Selfie keeps everything on your phone. We don't collect, upload, sell or share any data.

## What the app does
When someone enters an incorrect PIN, password or pattern on your phone, the app takes one photo with the front camera and stores it privately inside the app.

## Data and permissions
- **Camera:** used only to take the photo described above.
- **Device Administrator (watch login attempts):** used only so Android can notify the app of a failed unlock attempt. The app cannot lock, wipe or change your device.
- **Notifications:** used to tell you that a photo was captured. Notification contents are hidden on the lockscreen.
- **Photos:** stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not saved to your gallery, not uploaded, and not accessible to other apps. Viewing them requires your fingerprint, face or device PIN. You can delete them at any time in the app, and uninstalling the app deletes them.

## Data we collect
None. The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or network access, and nothing leaves your device.

## Your responsibility
Use the app only on a device you own. Laws about photographing people vary by country; you are responsible for using the app lawfully.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
